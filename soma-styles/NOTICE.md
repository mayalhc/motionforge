# SOMA styles

The `.npz` files here are the SOMA human's styles for the MotionForge add-on: short clips of
a person walking, jogging, crawling and so on (global rotations and positions at 30 fps).

They are fetched by the add-on's **Install Engines** into the engine folder and are checked
against `manifest.json` (size and SHA-256) before they are used.

The clips were generated with NVIDIA's Kimodo model, which is released under the
NVIDIA Open Model License. They are not NVIDIA's sample data.
